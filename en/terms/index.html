Key Terms

Foundational concepts this site's methodology relies on for verifying historical narrations

1 What Is the Sanad (Chain of Narration)? The chain of narrators connecting to the text, and the standard for accepting any historical report. 2 The Explicit Qur'anic Definition of "Ahl al-Bayt" (the Household) Interpreting the Qur'an by the Qur'an to determine who is meant by this term. 3 The Definition of "Aal" (Family/Followers) What the term means linguistically and religiously, between followers and blood relations. 4 Tadlees (Concealment) — The Slow Poison Concealing the intermediary between a narrator and the person he narrates from, using wording that implies direct transmission.
